# pbl__project
AI-powered algae air purification system with IoT-based pollution detection using ESP32 and air quality sensors.
PROJECT TITLE - OXYPOD SMART ALGAE POWERED AIR PURIFIER
Problem Statement -
Air Quality Index (AQI) levels in residential areas are continuously increasing due to sources such as vehicular emissions, construction dust, garbage burning, and industrial activities. While existing monitoring systems indicate that air quality is poor, they do not identify the exact cause or source of pollution in a specific locality. Traditional air purifiers are expensive, consume high electricity, and mainly filter indoor air without reducing carbon dioxide or addressing the root cause. There is a need for an affordable and intelligent system that can both detect the reason behind rising pollution and actively reduce it.
Solution - OxyPod uses:Air quality sensors , ESP32 microcontroller , Algae bioreactor chamber , Solar-powered system
Hardware Used: ESP32 , MQ135 Air Quality Sensor , PMS5003 Dust Sensor , DHT22 Temperature Sensor , CO₂ Sensor (MH-Z19) , LED Grow Light
software used: Arduino IDE , tinker card(for circuit design)
