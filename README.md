# 🌿 OxyPod – Smart Algae Powered Air Purifier

AI-powered algae air purification system with IoT-based pollution detection using ESP32 and air quality sensors.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Air Quality Index (AQI) levels in residential areas are continuously increasing due to vehicular emissions, construction dust, garbage burning, and industrial activities. Existing monitoring systems indicate poor air quality but do not identify the exact source of pollution in a specific locality. Traditional air purifiers are expensive, consume high electricity, and mainly filter indoor air without reducing carbon dioxide. There is a need for an affordable and intelligent system that can detect the cause of pollution and actively reduce it in a sustainable way.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

OxyPod integrates:
- Real-time air quality sensors  
- ESP32 microcontroller  
- Algae-based bioreactor chamber  
- Solar-powered energy system  

The system detects pollution sources and reduces CO₂ biologically while providing real-time monitoring.

---

## 🔧 Hardware Used

- ESP32(arduino UNO)
- MQ Gs sensor 
- Temperature & Humidity Sensor  
- Buzzer 
- LED Grow Light
