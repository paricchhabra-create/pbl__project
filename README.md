# pbl__project
AI-powered algae air purification system with IoT-based pollution detection using ESP32 and air quality sensors.
 OxyPod – AI-Powered Algae Air Purification System
## Problem Statement

Air Quality Index (AQI) levels in residential areas and schools are continuously increasing due to vehicular emissions, construction dust, garbage burning, and nearby industrial activities. Although AQI monitoring systems display pollution levels, they fail to identify the exact cause and localized source of pollution.

Traditional air purifiers:

* Consume high electricity
* Are expensive
* Primarily filter particulate matter
* Do not significantly reduce CO₂ levels
* Are not sustainable long-term

There is a need for an affordable, intelligent, and eco-friendly system that can both detect the cause of pollution and actively reduce it in a sustainable way.

---

##  Proposed Solution

**OxyPod** integrates real-time air monitoring with biological carbon capture technology.

The system includes:

* Real-time air quality sensors (Gas, Temperature, Humidity)
* ESP32 microcontroller for IoT connectivity
* Algae-based bioreactor chamber
* Solar-powered energy system
* LED grow light to enhance photosynthesis

###  Working Principle

1. Sensors continuously monitor air quality.
2. Data is processed by the ESP32.
3. If pollution levels exceed threshold:

   * Alerts are triggered (LED/Buzzer).
   * Air is directed into the algae chamber.
4. Microalgae absorb CO₂ via photosynthesis.
5. Oxygen-rich air is released back into the environment.
6. Solar panels power the system sustainably.

##  Results and Analysis

During prototype testing:

* The gas sensor successfully detected variations in pollution levels.
* The system triggered alerts when pollution crossed predefined thresholds.
* Sensor data monitoring demonstrated real-time environmental tracking capability.
* The integration logic between detection and purification mechanism was validated.

Analysis indicates that:

* Biological carbon capture using algae provides a sustainable alternative to traditional filtration systems.
* Solar-powered operation significantly reduces electricity dependency.
* Real-time IoT monitoring improves localized pollution management.

The prototype validates the feasibility of combining IoT-based pollution detection with algae-based CO₂ reduction.
